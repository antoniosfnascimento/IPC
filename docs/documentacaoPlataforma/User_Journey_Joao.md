# User Journey Map: João (Wheelchair profile)

**Persona:** João, 34. Manual wheelchair user. Values independence but suffers severe exhaustion on steep or uneven paths.
**Scenario:** João just got a new job in the city centre (unfamiliar to him) and needs to plan a daily route from the railway station to the office.

| Journey phase | Main action | João's thought | Emotion (curve) | UX/UI improvement opportunity on CityFlow |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Discovery and planning (pre-trip)** | João opens CityFlow and searches for the path between the station and the office. The map returns a route immediately. | *"This looks weird. Google Maps would send me straight down that big avenue — why is CityFlow taking me around the block?"* | **Neutral / curious** | **Visual route justification:** the app should draw the rejected main avenue as a faded red dashed line and overlay icons ("Stairs", "12% slope"). That educates João on *why* the algorithm spared him, building trust in the math engine instead of feeling like a navigation bug. |
| **2. Profile configuration** | Before leaving, João notices the area has cobblestone pavement. He opens the modal and aggressively blocks all irregular surfaces (`surface_preference = paved`). | *"Yesterday's rain will make my wheels slip on the cobblestone. I want to make sure I get ONLY asphalt or concrete, even if it means 500 m extra."* | **Confident / in control** | **Quick effort sliders:** instead of forcing João to pick obscure construction terms via checkboxes (e.g. *sett* vs *cobblestone*), turn it into a simple slider: "Pavement comfort level: 1 (Anything) to 5 (Perfect asphalt)". The frontend translates that into the OSM string arrays. |
| **3. Real-time navigation** | João walks along the pavement. When he approaches a turn he stops the wheelchair (braking both wheels) to pull out the phone and confirm the direction. | *"I have to stop the wheelchair at every complex intersection because I cannot push the wheels and look at the screen at the same time."* | **Frustrated / annoyed** | **Haptic and spatial audio feedback:** mandatory use of the Vibration API and TTS. When João is 10 m from the turn, the phone vibrates in his pocket (alert), and Bluetooth earbuds say "Turn left on the wide pavement". His hands never leave the wheel rims. |
| **4. Arrival** | João reaches the office building without cardiac stress or impassable obstacles. He ends the route on the bottom button. | *"It took 4 min more than the straight-line estimate, but I arrived without sweating. And I was never on the road. It worked."* | **Relieved / satisfied** | **Building access (terminal micro-routing):** CityFlow stopped on the "street". But where is the "accessible door"? A future improvement: "The ramp for your destination is on the left side of the building, 15 m away." |
